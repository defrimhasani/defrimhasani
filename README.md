## Useful software, out in the world

### [Selektos](https://github.com/defrimhasani/selektos)
**Your databases. A native Mac app.**

A macOS SQL client for PostgreSQL and Cloudflare D1. Workspaces, a native query editor, Keychain-backed credentials, and a built-in MCP server that gives AI agents controlled access to saved connections.

`Swift` · `SwiftUI` · `AppKit` · `PostgreSQL` · `MCP`

[Explore Selektos →](https://defrimhasani.github.io/selektos/) · [Read the code](https://github.com/defrimhasani/selektos)

### [Trackline](https://github.com/defrimhasani/trackline)
**Less friction between doing the work and logging it.**

A desktop timesheet for Jira Cloud. See your week, log time, find incomplete days, and export your own or your team's worklogs. Connects directly to Jira—no Trackline account or server.

`TypeScript` · `React` · `Tauri` · `Rust` · `Jira Cloud`

[Explore Trackline →](https://defrimhasani.github.io/trackline/) · [Download](https://github.com/defrimhasani/trackline/releases/latest) · [Read the code](https://github.com/defrimhasani/trackline)

### [Service Canvas](https://github.com/defrimhasani/service-canvas)
**Get the architecture out of your head.**

A browser-based canvas for mapping microservices. Drag services, connect them with protocol-labelled edges, and annotate the details. Diagrams stay in your browser's local storage; no account required.

`TypeScript` · `Next.js` · `React Flow` · `Zustand`

[Try the canvas →](https://service-canvas.vercel.app/) · [Read the code](https://github.com/defrimhasani/service-canvas)
